Three engines, each with its own rules

Each engine has its own entries, exits and magic number. They share one chart and one set of account guards.

Basket, long only

Buys gold at selected session hours, on short M1 pullbacks, on 6-minute rebounds and on strong 12-minute morning momentum. Layers have equal lots at fixed price steps, up to 10, never martingale. The whole basket closes at a profit target per ounce.

Breakout

Buy stop and sell stop orders at H1 swing levels and on the Asian session range, managed with a tight trailing stop. A regime switch keeps a shadow record of each module and sends real orders only while its recent results are positive.

Level

Stop orders at a ladder of recent daily highs and lows, with stop loss and take profit as a percentage of price and a trailing stop. One position at a time. A 30-day equity filter moves the engine to shadow mode while its recent results are negative.

How it protects the account

  • Trade-idea guard. When the floating loss of one direction reaches 20% of the balance, that side is closed and new entries in that direction pause for 60 minutes.
  • Daily guard. When equity falls 20% from the start of the trading day, all XAUrally positions and orders are closed until the next day.
  • Basket limits. A basket brake in USD per 0.01 lot and a maximum basket age cap every grid basket.
  • Order checks. Every request is checked against the broker's limits first: algo trading permission, session, volume, free margin, stop and freeze levels.

Risk warning

The Basket engine uses a grid (averaging) method: it adds positions while the price moves against the basket, and a basket can be closed at a loss by the basket brake, the trade-idea guard or the daily guard. The guards cannot act while the market is closed, so a weekend gap can produce a larger loss than the set limits.

Results of backtests and past trading do not guarantee future results. In backtests over several years, results depended strongly on the starting month: some 12-month periods ended with a loss, and calm, low-volatility markets produced losing months.

Trading leveraged gold carries a high level of risk and can result in the loss of your deposit. Test XAUrally on a demo account first, start with the default settings, and only trade money you can afford to lose.

Can I change the strategy parameters?

No. The inputs contain account settings only: which engines run, lots, broker server time, the account guards and the magic number. The strategy parameters are fixed, so every copy behaves like the tested version.

Is XAUrally suitable for prop-firm challenges?

It is not designed for them. Prop-firm daily loss limits are usually much tighter than the default guards, and tighter guards cut baskets early.

Can I run it next to manual trades or other EAs?

Use one chart per account and keep other activity away from the XAUUSD symbol. The daily guard measures the equity of the whole account.
