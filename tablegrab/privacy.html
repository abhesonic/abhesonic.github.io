Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

TableGrab is a Chrome extension that turns the tables and lists on web pages into rows and columns you can download or copy. This policy explains what information TableGrab handles and why. In short: TableGrab has no account, no analytics and no ads, and it never sends your browsing data or the data you collect to us.

1. Information processed on your device

  • Content of the pages you ask TableGrab to read. When you click the TableGrab icon, TableGrab reads the tables and lists of that page (their text, link addresses and image addresses) and the page title and address, so it can show them as rows and columns. When you collect more pages, it reads each following page in the same way, and with "Add the same data from other tabs of this site" it reads your other open tabs of that website. This happens only inside your browser. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The files you save. CSV, Excel and JSON files are created on your computer and saved to your Downloads folder. "Copy" puts the rows on your clipboard.
  • Settings and saved setups. Your options (such as the CSV separator) are stored in Chrome's extension storage on your device. Pro users can let TableGrab remember the setup for a website: the website's name (for example shop.example.com), the column choices and names, and a short description of that site's Next button. These setups stay on your device and are removed when you choose "Forget it" or uninstall TableGrab.

2. Information sent to a third party: Pro license checks

If you buy TableGrab Pro, the purchase is handled by Gumroad, which acts as the merchant of record. Gumroad processes your payment and order details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details.

When you activate Pro, TableGrab sends your license key and the TableGrab Pro product ID to the Gumroad License API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Every few days TableGrab sends the license key again to confirm that the license is still valid (for example, that the purchase was not refunded). TableGrab stores the license key and the product ID on your device. It does not store your name or email address.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your browsing history, the content of the pages you visit or the data you collect.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or remote code.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose other than TableGrab's single purpose: turning the tables and lists you choose into spreadsheet data.
  • We do not use or transfer data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

The use of information by TableGrab complies with the Chrome Web Store Program Policies, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to read the tables and lists of the page you are viewing, only after you click the TableGrab icon, and to follow its Next button when you ask for more pages.
  • sidePanel: to show TableGrab in Chrome's side panel next to the page.
  • storage: to remember your settings, saved website setups and Pro license on your device.
  • Access to api.gumroad.com: to activate and check a Pro license.
  • Optional access to websites: requested only when you choose it, for example "Always allow on this site", "Add the same data from other tabs of this site", or when a next page is on a different website. Each request covers that one website. You can withdraw it at any time in Chrome's extension settings.

5. Data retention and your choices

All data TableGrab keeps stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the extension. To remove your license from a browser, open TableGrab settings (gear icon) and choose "Remove license from this browser". For questions about your purchase data, contact Gumroad or us.

6. Children

TableGrab is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
