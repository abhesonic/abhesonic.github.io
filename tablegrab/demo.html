TableGrab is installed

  1. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to TableGrab.
  2. Try it here: click the TableGrab icon. A panel opens on the right with the lamps below as rows and columns.
  3. More pages: in the panel, press Find it and then Start to collect all three pages.
  4. Save: press CSV or Copy and paste into your spreadsheet.
